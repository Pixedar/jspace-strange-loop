# Publication scope

Wiktor Tomasik · measurements: 1 November 2026 · publication updated: 2026-11-01T23:52:27+01:00

This edition contains one selected reader checkpoint, a portable recorded demo, research explanations, figures and compact measurements. The GitHub repository and Hugging Face model, dataset and Space are private at release time. The personal website hosts the public, sanitized explorer. Repository visibility can change independently; the measurements and their dates do not.

Software, the interactive viewer and compact reader weights are licensed AGPL-3.0-only. New author-owned reports, figures and numerical observations are CC BY-SA 4.0. Earlier releases retain their original licenses. Upstream model weights are not bundled. See LICENSING.md and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

The external WikiText, AG News, TinyStories and DialogSum passages are not redistributed in this edition. Their token strings are omitted from the passage explorer; authored tasks, model responses and numerical paths remain. Saved controller histories contain numerical internal observations and learning statistics derived from that corpus. Their inclusion does not relicense any source text or base model. Raw token-bearing recordings and the full development archive remain outside this release.

The evidence concerns persistent selection, prediction of attention, ownership-sensitive interventions and controlled effects on language-model processing. Recognized causal ownership, general personality and subjective experience are not established. Model-generated experimental responses and model-based exploratory scores are identified in the methods; exploratory semantic ratings are not human-validated measurements.

Configuration and measurement files preserve their experiment timestamps. The publication manifest records the packaging timestamp and file hashes. Account credentials, rental controls and machine-specific execution logs are excluded.
