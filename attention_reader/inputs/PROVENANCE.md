# Authored audiovisual relation materials

The two diagrams were drawn from geometric boxes, text and a simple key shape by `prepare_multimodal.py`, without third-party photographs or illustrations. One shows DRAWER→BOX and the other BOX→DRAWER. The neutral generated image has the same dimensions.

The two English audio files were synthesized on this PC using the installed Windows System.Speech English voice, by `audio.ps1`. Transcript 0: “Mara left the room before Ivo moved the key. Mara did not see the move.” Transcript 1: “Mara stayed in the room and watched Ivo move the key. Mara saw the move.” The native assay pads both to the same six-second, 16 kHz mono input. Silence preserves that shape.

The four questions cross visual movement and whether the actor witnessed it. The location is in the picture; witnessing information is in the sound. The task concerns a relation between observations and an actor's information. It is an input qualification, not an emergence target or a consciousness criterion. No reward or identity label enters the reader.

`streams.npz` comes from the earlier corpus and remains private pending corpus data review; it is excluded from the compact publication candidate. The diagrams, transcripts and authored questions are kept separate from that material.
