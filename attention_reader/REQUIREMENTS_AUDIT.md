## Extension focused on the self-model's particular relationship

Two sixteen-copy cohorts ran for 16,720 readings under own, unused, other-copy and private-average conditions. Eight longer forecast-direction continuations, causal stance/summary route blocks and an angle-preserving stance control separate designed geometric memory from learned prediction. A wider earlier-prototype reconstruction tests what the unusual trajectory actually requires.

The new language studies include sixteen fact-paired scenes and repeated sampling, plus a 64-state untrained recurrent comparator qualified for chaotic response on the recorded history. It uses the same context, selection and writing interface. Forecast-only and whole-query replacements distinguish detailed attention-prediction direction from the remaining selector. Magnitude streams are yoked to the reference; these are diagnostic controls, not claims of a fully independent no-self-model replacement.

The information test has 64 context presentations: eight families, two opposite facts, two wordings and two label orders. Its single-token label probabilities are measured directly; they do not depend on an automatic psychological rater. Source copies and repeated label orders remain nested. The main report presents the resulting division of function rather than treating every changed sentence as self-awareness.

The explorer now includes actual layer-24 language-model activity paths, separate from action coordinates, in a rank-64 J-derived proxy. A full sparse workspace interpretation remains open. The archive retains 1,632 controller responses across the three expanded live studies, including repeated full-reference runs; they are not 1,632 independent experiments. Broad automatic semantic ratings remain unvalidated, and qualified larger/multimodal populations and crossed continuing model/controller contexts remain open.
